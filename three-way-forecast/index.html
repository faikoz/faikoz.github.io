What the forecast rests on

Assumptions

  • • Sales grow about 10%: a full year of the Mackay depot, plus a mid-year price rise
  • • Cost of sales at 66.5% of sales (65.4% last year); two supplier agreements are up for renewal
  • • Overheads stay close to flat in dollars, so they fall as a share of sales
  • • No large purchases: only vehicle and forklift replacements already scheduled
  • • The bank rate stays where it is for the year
  • • Stock and debtors grow in line with sales, no faster

What could go wrong

  • • Cash is thin in Jul, Dec and Jan
  • • Bank lending of $1.48M (6.9% of sales) has to be serviced whatever the month
  • • A weaker dollar lifts the cost of the imported ranges
  • • Building and mining both slow at the same time
  • • Debtors of $2.72M (about 46 days of sales) drifting later
  • • Discounting to hold project work eats the margin

Suggested next step: arrange a $0.45M overdraft to cover the lean months, and chase debtors a week earlier so less of it is needed.
